abstract: >
  CETERO-S is a semiautomatic Python tool for the analysis
  of J–R resistance curves from compact tension C(T) fracture
  tests using the unloading compliance method. The software
  combines automated numerical procedures with supervised
  user interaction, allowing traceable and auditable analysis
  workflows instead of black-box processing. CETERO-S enables
  inspection and selection of compliance candidates, rotation
  correction, crack growth estimation and ASTM E1820-inspired
  post-processing.
